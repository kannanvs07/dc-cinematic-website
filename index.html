OVERVIEW

THE STORY BEHIND DC

Reimagining Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay's classic novel Devdas alongside shades of Bonnie and Clyde, DC: The Bloody Valentine enters a gritty contemporary world ruled by guns, cops, and gangsters.

The story follows Devadas (Lokesh Kanagaraj), a rebellious outlaw on the run from a ruthless police force who gets pulled into a violent spiral of romance, vengeance, and redemption as Chandra (Wamiqa Gabbi) and Parvathy (Sanjana Krishnamoorthy) enter his life.

Directed by Arun Matheswaran and produced by Sun Pictures, the film delivers cathartic action, striking cinematography by Mukesh G, and a pulsating electronic-fusion background score by Anirudh Ravichander.
